The goal was set at 0.80 under the held-out-query protocol — the standard link-prediction setup, and the reading of the spec chosen at kickoff. That bar is cleared at 0.836, with precision@1 of 0.920 and MRR of 0.958. The bare number is reported with equal prominence because it is the one that describes the model's own reasoning.

What was built

Four stages, one A10G

01

Represent

Each of 13,036 anime becomes a ~2,900-dimension vector: a frozen Qwen3-Embedding-4B encoding of title, genres, themes, studio and synopsis, concatenated with 256 ALS co-watch factors learned from 115.8M user interactions, plus metadata multi-hots.

02

Retrieve

A 3-to-9-seed two-tower ensemble — small MLPs trained with in-batch sampled softmax on vote-weighted recommendation pairs — proposes 250 candidates, unioned with co-occurrence, content and graph neighbours. This pool already contains 89–96% of the correct answers.

03

Rank

A LightGBM LambdaRank model orders the pool over 24 features, trained five-fold-honest so no training query's features come from a model that saw its edges. This stage is where the accuracy is won or lost.

04

Serve

A CLI and an HTTP service with a mode switch that exposes all three regimes above, so any recommendation can be inspected with the crowd data switched off. Franchise filtering removes the query's own sequels.

Why the ceiling is real

The candidate pool holds 89% of the correct answers, and a perfect ordering of that same pool would score 0.996. So the barrier is not retrieval, representation, or model capacity — it is that which ten of thirty equally plausible anime a community canonised is social and historical information, not a property of the shows. Code Geass became the Death Note recommendation in 2008 and self-reinforced; nothing in a synopsis or a watch history distinguishes it from ten equally apt thrillers.

Two things move the number, and both are the same move: import an answer. Adding AniList's independent community graph lifted the strict regime from 0.508 to 0.776. Adding the crowd's own list reaches 0.836 and beyond.

What remains

  • The goal is met under the held-out-query protocol; a decision to judge it under the strict protocol instead would leave it 0.024 short, and the bare protocol is capped near 0.50 on the evidence above.
  • Modelling work is closed. Every remaining idea from the literature survey was tested or eliminated at a pre-registered gate.
  • Product work continues: catalogue coverage beyond 13,036 titles, and a permanent address for the demo in place of an ephemeral tunnel.
